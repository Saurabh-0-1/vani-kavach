About the project

Make the signal
the starting point.

Vani Kavach is an audio investigation workspace for examining potentially synthetic or manipulated voice recordings with clear evidence and careful limitations.

Why it matters

Voice evidence deserves context.

Synthetic speech and manipulated recordings can make familiar voices harder to assess. Vani Kavach focuses first on the signal: validating the file, standardizing its audio, and presenting what the pipeline can actually observe.

The project is intended for learners, investigators, and teams exploring responsible voice-security workflows. It is not a guarantee of authenticity and should not replace human review or forensic procedures.

Current workflow

01UploadSelect a supported recording.
02ValidateCheck format, duration, energy, and metadata.
03AnalyzeRun the available experimental detector.
04ReviewRead results together with their limitations.

Technology

Built around a real API.

The repository uses Python, FastAPI, Uvicorn, NumPy, SciPy, SoundFile, Librosa, Keras, and pytest. The browser interface is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, connected to the FastAPI validation and analysis routes.

Honest scope

What exists today.

Audio preprocessing and metadata validation are available. The detector may run in experimental mode when the model is loaded, but its output is uncalibrated and not validated for real-world scam or deepfake detection. Accounts, authentication, and contextual risk assessment are not implemented.

PLANNED, NOT YET AVAILABLE

Future goals include secure accounts, richer investigation history, and additional evaluated models. These are plans, not current capabilities.
